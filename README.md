# Pour Decisions

A personal wine inventory for your home fridge or cellar. Keep track of what you have, which shelf it's on, what you paid, and what you've opened.

**Open the app:** https://fivebarn.github.io/pour-decisions/

> Pour Decisions is in beta. It's built for tracking your own bottles at home. It doesn't sell, buy, or trade wine, and it isn't connected to any winery or store.

## What it does

- **Inventory:** vintage, brand, varietal, price, notes, tags, and bottle count for every wine.
- **Shelves:** set up your fridge (how many shelves, how many bottles each holds) and see what's on each shelf and how much room is left.
- **Scan to drink:** scan a bottle's barcode with your phone camera to take one bottle out of your inventory. If two vintages share a barcode, it asks which one you opened.
- **On order:** log bottles you've bought but haven't picked up yet. They don't count until you mark them received.
- **Cellar audit:** count every bottle once in a while and fix anything that's off.
- **Totals and export:** bottle counts by varietal, brand, and shelf, plus CSV export for Excel or Google Sheets.

## Install it on your phone

It runs in your phone's browser. No app store and no account needed.

**iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
**Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

It then opens full screen like a regular app and works offline.

## Your data

- Everything you enter is stored **only on your phone**, in the browser. There are no accounts, and nothing is sent to a server.
- Nobody else can see your cellar, including the people who made the app.
- Because it lives on your device, clearing your browser data or deleting the app removes your cellar. Use **Export inventory → Download CSV** now and then as a backup. You can bring it back with **Import a Pour Decisions CSV**.
- On iPhone, add it to your Home Screen. Safari can clear data for websites you haven't opened in a few weeks, but Home Screen apps are kept.
- The camera is used only to read barcodes while the scanner is open. No photos are saved or uploaded.
